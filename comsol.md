1. Go to https://systems.engr.ucr.edu/welcome and click on **My Account**, then follow the steps to set up your ENGR account.  Note that this ENGR account is BCOE-specific and different from your main UCR NetID that you use for most things.  You might have already set up your ENGR account during orientation.

> If you already have an ENGR account but you've forgotten your username or password**, the same site can help you reset your password.

> If you're not a BCOE student**, you'll probably need to go to the BCOE Systems office to ask them set up an account for you - they are located down the hallway to the left of the Bytes cafe on the first floor of in Winston Chung Hall.  Tell them that you're not a BCOE student but you're enrolled in BIEN 010 and you need access to COMSOL Multiphysics for the class.  They should be able to set you up with an ENGR account.

1. After your ENGR account is successfully set up, **wait for at least an hour before trying to install COMSOL** to give the system time to make the necessary connections between your ENGR and UCR accounts.  While you're waiting, you can...

1. Download the COMSOL installer for your computer.  This is a LARGE download so do this somewhere that you have a good reliable internet connection (and/or start the download before bed and let it run overnight).  Download the COMSOL installer that matches your computer:

> Windows users:  COMSOL63_dvd.isoLinks to an external site.

> Mac users with newer Apple Silicon processors:  COMSOL63_full_macarm64.dmgLinks to an external site.

> Mac users with older Intel processors:  COMSOL63_full_maci64.dmgLinks to an external site.
